COMMUNITY RESPECT PLEDGE
Respectful Neighbor
Considerate Camper
Striving to live outdoors with dignity, accountability, and respect for our shared neighborhood.

Our commitments

  • Cleanliness & waste: Trash is bagged and disposed of properly. The surrounding area stays free of loose litter.
  • Clear access: Sidewalks, curb ramps, doorways, fire lanes, and paths stay open.
  • Quiet & peace: Noise stays low, especially 10:00 PM–7:00 AM unless a different local rule applies.
  • Safety: No unsafe flames or hazardous cooking setups. We cooperate about immediate safety concerns.
  • Communication: Polite, direct concerns are welcome when safe. No threats, harassment, filming, or taking belongings.

Status: Active participant in peer standards

Optional mutual-aid / advocate reference:

Note to neighbors: This is a voluntary good-neighbor signal—not a lease, property permission, legal defense, or safety guarantee. Do not post a person’s name, phone, recovery code, medical information, or exact location online. For urgent danger contact emergency services; for other concerns use respectful communication or local outreach.
