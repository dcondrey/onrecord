2. Shared conditions

  • Keep entrances, sidewalks, curb ramps, fire lanes, exits, utilities, and customer access clear.
  • Keep the area reasonably clean; use the agreed trash, bathroom, water, and sharps-disposal locations.
  • Keep noise, visitors, cooking, pets, and belongings within the agreed limits.
  • Do not damage property, threaten people, or interfere with business operations.
  • The representative should give reasonable notice before ending permission when safety allows.
