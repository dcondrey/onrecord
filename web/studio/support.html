Support

Questions, bug reports, and feature requests for OnRecord Studio.

Open an issue: github.com/dcondrey/onrecord/issues. A free GitHub account is required to post. Replies usually within two business days.

What to include

Your macOS version, the OnRecord Studio version from the About window, and what you were doing when the problem happened. If a document failed to parse, name the format. Do not attach the document itself, and do not paste anything from a records response you would not publish: issues are public.

Common questions

Where is my data?

In a local database file inside the app's container on your Mac. Nothing is uploaded. There is no account to create and no server to sign in to.

Which statutes are supported?

Deadline evaluation implements the California Public Records Act only. Collection, parsing, and evidence handling work on records from anywhere.

The app says it cannot verify my purchase.

Open the Mac App Store and sign in with the Apple Account that bought the app, then press "Verify purchase again". OnRecord Studio is a one-time paid app: there is no subscription, and no license key to enter.

Can a document be un-redacted?

No. The app audits a released document for residual and recoverable content and reports what it finds. It does not reconstruct redacted text.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Security reports

Do not open a public issue for a security vulnerability. Follow the security policy.
