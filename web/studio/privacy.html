Privacy Policy

OnRecord Studio for macOS. Last updated 28 August 2026.

What is collected

Nothing. The app has no account system, no server component, and no third-party analytics or crash-reporting service. The developer receives no usage data, no diagnostics, and no copy of anything you open.

Where your data lives

Documents you ingest, the text extracted from them, and every finding the app produces are stored in database files inside the app's sandbox container on your Mac. Deleting the app removes them. Exported reports and evidence packages are written only to locations you choose.

Network activity

The app connects to the internet in exactly two cases: to fetch records from the portals and URLs you supply, and to verify your purchase with Apple. Requests for records go to the operators of the sites you name, and their own privacy practices govern them.

Purchase verification

The app asks Apple's StoreKit whether this copy was purchased, and Apple returns a signed transaction. That exchange is between your Mac and Apple. The developer does not receive it and cannot see who bought the app.

Support requests

Support runs through public GitHub issues. Anything you post there is public and governed by GitHub's privacy policy. Nothing in the app sends anything to GitHub on your behalf.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

Material changes will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue.
