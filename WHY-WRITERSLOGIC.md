# Why On Record is under WritersLogic

On Record is hosted under the WritersLogic organization because WritersLogic is the organization led
by its founder and CEO, David Condrey. David lived without stable housing for several years and still
has close friends who are homeless. He offers support when he can while also recognizing that support
must not require sacrificing his safety, boundaries, or ability to continue helping.

That history is part of the project’s origin—not a claim to speak for every person who is homeless, and
not a substitute for lived-experience leadership. The project must keep listening to people who live
outdoors now, people who have moved into housing, advocates, providers, businesses, and public officials.
No single person’s experience represents an entire community.

## Why an organization matters

WritersLogic provides a stable home for the code, documentation, security reporting, stewardship, and
long-term accountability. It also makes the responsibility visible: the organization must explain what
it collects, protect people from exposure, accept correction, and stop features that create harm.

The organization does not own anyone’s story. A request belongs first to the person who made it. Hosting
the project does not authorize WritersLogic, its CEO, contributors, providers, or the public to identify,
contact, photograph, investigate, or speak for that person.

## Our obligation

Lived experience should change design decisions, not become a marketing credential. We will seek consent,
share power with people directly affected, compensate expertise where possible, publish limitations, and
keep a clear distinction between a prototype and a service people can safely rely on.

If the project ever makes people more visible without making them safer, more respected, or better able
to get help, WritersLogic should narrow it, pause it, or stop it.
