# Resources and printables

## San Diego starting points

- **2-1-1 San Diego:** current referrals for shelter/intake, food, hygiene, storage, transport, and
  outreach. Verify hours, eligibility, and capacity before traveling.
- **911:** immediate danger or life-threatening emergency.
- **988:** mental-health or suicide crisis; ask about language access and mobile response.
- **Neil Good Day Center:** city day services including restrooms, laundry, charging, phones, computers,
  and mail; confirm current hours at the City’s service page.
- **Street medicine:** Family Health Centers Street Medicine, (619) 515-2300; confirm schedule.

The directory is a verify-before-travel guide, not a live bed feed or acceptance guarantee.
