# Commitments and printables

These materials are voluntary tools for communication and self-governance. None is a lease, tenancy,
private-property authorization, legal defense, safety guarantee, or protection from enforcement.

## Respectful Neighbor Commitment

The occupant voluntarily commits to bag and dispose of waste, keep sidewalks/curb ramps/doorways/fire
lanes clear, reduce noise during agreed quiet hours, avoid unsafe flames, secure pets, and communicate
about concerns when safe. The placard and QR sticker point to this standard. Do not put a name, phone,
recovery code, medical information, or exact location on them.

## Private-property permission

A business/property representative may document a specific area, dates, conditions, access to agreed
facilities, and copies retained by both sides. The signer must have authority; the form does not override
zoning, fire, health, accessibility, employment, insurance, landlord, or other legal requirements.

## Mutual-aid agreement

Groups may write voluntary commitments for supplies, waste/sharps, quiet hours, privacy, and conflict
pauses. No one must join, disclose a diagnosis, surrender belongings, or accept unsafe mediation. Threats,
violence, fire, weapons, exploitation, and medical emergencies require safety and appropriate emergency or
outreach response.

## Print safely

Keep health cards and document trackers private. Verify service hours and rules before travel. Print in
large type when possible, offer translations and read-aloud support, and provide an accessible digital
alternative.
