# Moderation automation

The project uses automation to help humans notice risk, not to decide who belongs. GitHub workflows can
run when a discussion or discussion comment is created or edited. On Record’s workflow adds review labels
for simple signals such as link floods, scam language, threats, privacy exposure, dehumanizing language,
or obvious character floods.

## Safeguards

- No automatic deletion, hiding, locking, banning, or suspension.
- No third-party AI moderation service receives discussion text.
- No penalty for disagreement, anger, poverty, disability, or criticism.
- Every signal requires human context review and may be a false positive.
- Reviewers preserve only necessary evidence and provide a private appeal route.
- It does not expose the suspected content in a public bot reply.

Review the signal list quarterly with lived-experience and accessibility advisors. Add a signal only with
a harm example, false-positive test, owner, and rollback plan.
