# Limitations

The viewer has no live backend, no production provider authentication, no durable audit log, no real
client records, and no public live bed inventory. Its signatures prove byte integrity—not truth,
authorization, consent, or service quality. Do not enter protected client information into the demo.

Read the complete [limitations document](https://github.com/writerslogic/onrecord/blob/main/docs/limitations.md) before adapting the code.
