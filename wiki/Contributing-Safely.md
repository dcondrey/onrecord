# Contributing safely

Before proposing a field, metric, provider integration, identity workflow, or moderation rule, explain:

- who benefits and who could be harmed;
- what is collected, where it is stored, who can see it, and when it is deleted;
- how a person declines, corrects, withdraws, appeals, or restores access;
- what happens when the system is wrong, unavailable, compromised, or abused; and
- how the change will be tested with lived-experience participants, low-cost devices, and assistive tech.

Use synthetic fixtures. Never commit real client data, names, DOBs, ZIPs, recovery cards, credentials,
private keys, or screenshots containing protected information. For a security vulnerability, use the
private security-reporting process rather than a public discussion.
