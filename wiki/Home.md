# On Record Wiki

On Record is a prototype for consented, de-identified community requests with independently
verifiable provenance. It is designed around a practical problem: a person may need to return to a
request without a reliable device, email address, username, or password. It is not an HMIS,
coordinated-entry system, shelter-placement service, emergency line, medical record, or live bed
dashboard.

## Start here

1. **Understand the site:** [About On Record](About-On-Record), [Limitations](Limitations), and
   [Aspiration and roadmap](Aspiration-and-Roadmap).
2. **Choose your audience:** [General public](For-the-General-Public), [People living outdoors](For-People-Living-Outdoors),
   or [Service providers and government](For-Providers-and-Government).
3. **Use the practical toolkit:** [Commitments and printables](Commitments-and-Printables),
   [Resources](Resources-and-Printables), and [Know Your Rights](Know-Your-Rights).
4. **For maintainers:** use the wiki as the reader-facing handbook. Repository `docs/` contains
   implementation references, templates, and source material; changes to public guidance should be
   reflected here with an owner and review date.

## The promise

The prototype keeps a request, its consent record, its original wording, its disclosed transformation,
and its integrity proof together. A signature can show that bytes changed; it cannot show that a story
is true, that consent was freely given, that a provider is authorized, or that a service is available.

## Safety rule

Never put a real person’s name, date of birth, ZIP code, health information, recovery credential,
immigration information, victim-service information, or precise sleeping location into the demo or a
public discussion. Use composite examples only. Call 911 for immediate danger, 988 for a mental-health
or suicide crisis, and 2-1-1 San Diego for current local service referrals.
