# Know Your Rights: California and San Diego orientation

**Educational information, not legal advice. Laws, orders, local rules, and practices change.** If you
are in immediate danger, prioritize safety. For legal help, contact qualified legal aid or an attorney.

## Interacting with law enforcement

Stay as safe as possible, keep hands visible, and do not physically resist a search. You can ask,
“Am I free to go?” If detained, ask why. You generally may remain silent; say clearly that you are
exercising that right. If arrested, ask for a lawyer and avoid discussing the facts until counsel is
available. Rules differ for drivers, borders, probation, and search conditions. ACLU SoCal’s
[When stopped by an officer](https://www.aclusocal.org/know-your-rights/when-stopped-officer/) guide is a
useful current reference.

## Sweeps, citations, and property

Read the City’s current [Unsafe Camping Ordinance information](https://www.sandiego.gov/police/services/neighborhood-policing-division/unsafe-camping)
and [abatement procedure](https://www.sandiego.gov/homelessness-strategies-and-solutions/services/abatement).
When safe, record the notice, date, time, location, agency, property-storage instructions, and contact.
Move medication, IDs, paperwork, mobility devices, and irreplaceable items first if possible. Ask for an
inventory/receipt and disability accommodation. Do not fight a crew. A citation is not proof of guilt;
do not ignore its court date or instructions.

## Public services and discrimination

Write down what happened, who was involved, witnesses, dates, and requested accommodation. Keep copies
privately and avoid posting names or locations. Ask a legal-aid advocate about a complaint to the
[California Civil Rights Department](https://calcivilrights.ca.gov/contactus/), [California Department of
Social Services](https://www.cdss.ca.gov/inforesources/reporting/file-a-complaint/discrimination-complaints),
or [LawHelpCA](https://www.lawhelpca.org/), depending on the issue.

## If rights may have been violated

1. Get safe; seek medical care for injury.
2. Preserve notices, receipts, photos, names, and witness contacts safely.
3. Ask an advocate or legal-aid provider before posting publicly.
4. Ask about deadlines, property retrieval, accommodations, and complaint routes.
5. Do not put protected details into On Record or a public discussion.
