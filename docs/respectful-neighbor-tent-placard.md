# Respectful Neighbor / Considerate Camper Commitment

> ## COMMUNITY RESPECT PLEDGE
> *Striving to live outdoors with dignity, accountability, and respect for our shared neighborhood.*

The occupant(s) of this space have voluntarily agreed to these community standards:

- **Daily cleanliness and waste control:** Trash is bagged securely and taken to an agreed disposal
  location. The immediate area is kept free of loose debris, litter, and discarded items.
- **Clear pathways and access:** Sidewalks, curb ramps, doorways, fire lanes, and pedestrian paths stay
  open and unobstructed.
- **Quiet and peace:** Noise is kept low, especially during the proposed quiet hours of **10:00 PM to
  7:00 AM**, unless a different local rule or agreement applies.
- **Safety and cooperation:** No unsafe open flames or hazardous cooking setups. The occupants cooperate
  with neighbors and property representatives about immediate safety concerns.
- **Open communication:** Polite, direct concerns are welcome when it is safe to raise them. Threats,
  harassment, filming, or taking belongings are not acceptable ways to raise a concern.
