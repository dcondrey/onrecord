# San Diego emergency resource and direct-aid directory

**Pocket guide — verify before traveling.** Hours, eligibility, intake rules, addresses, and capacity
change. Call **2-1-1** or visit [211 San Diego](https://211sandiego.org/) for the current service match.
Call **911** for immediate danger or a life-threatening emergency. Call or text **988** for a mental
health or suicide crisis; ask for language access or a mobile response option.

## Food and meals

Start with **2-1-1** and ask for “free meals,” “food pantry,” “CalFresh enrollment,” or a location
serving your current neighborhood. Food schedules change frequently; do not rely on an old printed list.
The [San Diego Food Bank](https://www.sandiegofoodbank.org/) and [Feeding San Diego](https://feedingsandiego.org/)
maintain searchable food resources and partner distributions.

## Bathrooms, showers, hygiene, and storage

Ask Neil Good Day Center about current restrooms, laundry, phones, charging, mail, and computer access.
Ask 2-1-1 for mobile showers, public restrooms, laundry, storage centers, syringe services, hygiene
supplies, and pet-friendly options. Ask what identification, appointment, or queue rules apply before
traveling.

## Documents and benefits

- [San Diego County Birth Certificates](https://www.sandiegocounty.gov/hhsa/programs/phs/office_of_vital_records_and_statistics/birth_certificates.html):
  5530 Overland Ave, Suite 170, San Diego, CA 92123 · (619) 692-5733. The County page describes a
  fee-exempt process for people experiencing homelessness; ask for the current affidavit and required
  proof before traveling.
- Ask 2-1-1 or an advocate for the nearest DMV, Social Security office, veterans service officer, legal
  aid, mail service, and document storage option. Requirements vary by document and identity history.

## Safety and privacy

Do not put a person’s name, medical information, recovery card, or exact location in a public post. This
directory is a referral starting point, not a live bed inventory or guarantee that a provider can accept
someone. Keep a paper copy, but check the source page or call before relying on it.
