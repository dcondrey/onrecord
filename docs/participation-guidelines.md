# Participation guidelines for the website and Discussions

This is the short, reader-facing version of [the community standards](../COMMUNITY-STANDARDS.md).

## Before you post

Ask: Is this useful? Is it kind? Is it necessary? Could it identify or endanger someone? Would I want it
said about me when I could not reply? If the answer is uncertain, remove the detail or ask a moderator
privately.

## Good contributions

Share a question, a practical resource, an accessibility improvement, a reproducible bug, a careful
disagreement, or a specific offer of help. Keep offers voluntary and never require a person to reveal
their identity, trauma, health, immigration status, or location.

## Prohibited contributions

No spam, hate, harassment, threats, doxxing, stalking, sexual exploitation, impersonation, scams,
malware, targeted political manipulation, or publication of private records. Do not post precise
locations or attempt to identify a composite/demo story as a real person.

## If you are frustrated

Pause before replying. The goal is not to win a comment thread; it is to leave the next person safer than
you found them. Describe the concrete problem, suggest a remedy, and leave room for someone else’s safety
constraints. If the thread becomes harmful, stop engaging and report it privately.

The current static demo does not accept public posts. These rules apply to project spaces now and to any
future interactive deployment.
