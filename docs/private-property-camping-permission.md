# Private Property Camping Permission

**Print this form. Complete it together. Keep one copy with the person receiving permission and one with
the business/property representative.**

This is a voluntary permission agreement for a specific private property. It is not a lease, tenancy,
shelter placement, or promise of housing. It does not override zoning, fire, health, accessibility,
employment, insurance, landlord, or other legal requirements. The representative signing must have
authority from the owner or lawful property manager. Either side may ask an advocate or attorney to
review it before signing.

## 2. Shared conditions

The person receiving permission agrees to:

- keep entrances, sidewalks, curb ramps, fire lanes, exits, utilities, and customer access clear;
- keep the approved area reasonably clean and place trash in the agreed container or location;
- use bathrooms, water, waste, and sharps disposal as agreed; never burn trash or leave sharps exposed;
- keep noise, visitors, cooking, pets, and belongings within the agreed limits;
- not damage property, threaten people, or interfere with business operations;
- contact the representative or advocate if the arrangement becomes unsafe or cannot be followed; and
- leave or move belongings when permission ends, unless a new written agreement is made.

The business/property representative agrees to:

- identify the exact approved area and any areas that must remain clear;
- explain access to bathrooms, water, trash, and emergency exits when those are available;
- give reasonable notice before ending permission when safety allows; and
- not use this form to demand protected medical, immigration, or other unnecessary personal information.

### Important

This form does not guarantee that police, inspectors, neighbors, a landlord, or another government body
will treat the location as authorized. If the signer is not the owner or lawful controller, the form may
not be effective. For questions about property rights, zoning, fire/safety rules, or a citation, contact
local legal aid. Do not publish this form, the person’s name, or the exact camping location on a public
map without informed consent and a safety review.
