# Living Outdoors with Respect

This is practical, non-punitive guidance—not a substitute for local rules, outreach advice, or a safe
place to stay. Conditions and services change; verify before traveling.

## Choosing a place

- Prefer a location where outreach workers can find you and where you are not blocking a doorway,
  sidewalk, curb ramp, fire lane, transit stop, school entrance, or emergency access.
- Avoid unstable slopes, flood channels, railroad corridors, active construction, exposed heat, and
  places where a vehicle cannot reach an emergency.
- Do not camp on private property without permission. Do not share another person’s location publicly.
- Keep a clear path out. Store belongings so they do not create trip hazards, and keep cooking or flames
  far from tents and dry materials.

## Waste and sharps

- Bag trash and ask outreach teams, sanitation workers, libraries, day centers, or service sites where
  disposal is available. Never burn trash.
- Put needles and other sharps in a rigid, closed container; do not recap, bend, or leave them on the
  ground. Ask a syringe-service or health site for safe disposal and supplies.
- Keep food sealed when possible and avoid feeding wildlife. Clean spills promptly.

## Hygiene and health

Use public bathrooms, showers, handwashing, drinking-water, laundry, and charging sites when available.
Wash hands before eating and after handling waste. Keep wounds covered when possible. Ask a clinic or
outreach worker about heat illness, infection, naloxone, safer-use supplies, and urgent care. Do not
share medication or medical information publicly.

## Noise, pets, and neighbors

Keep music and conversations low during quiet hours. Secure pets, clean up after them, and ask about
service-site rules. Acknowledge neighbors who need a clear path without accepting insults or threats.
If conflict is escalating, leave if it is safe and contact outreach rather than confronting someone.

## Finding services

Look for day centers, libraries, outreach teams, healthcare-for-the-homeless sites, syringe services,
restrooms, showers, food programs, storage, charging, and coordinated-entry hubs. On Record’s directory
is a sourced reference, not a live bed inventory. Call the provider before traveling; availability,
eligibility, hours, and intake rules may change.
