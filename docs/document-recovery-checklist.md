# Document Recovery Checklist and Tracker

Keep this private. Use an advocate, day center, legal-aid provider, veterans service officer, or 2-1-1
to confirm current requirements before traveling. Do not put document numbers in a public On Record
entry.

## First steps

- ☐ Ask an advocate to inventory what is missing without publishing the details.
- ☐ Choose a safe mail destination: day center, trusted person, provider mail service, or post office
  General Delivery if accepted and arranged in advance.
- ☐ Ask for fee waivers, homeless-status affidavits, transportation, translation, and disability
  accommodation before the appointment.
- ☐ Photograph or copy new documents only with secure, private storage.

## Birth certificate

- ☐ Identify the state/county of birth and whether an authorized or informational copy is needed.
- ☐ For San Diego County births, review [County Vital Records](https://www.sandiegocounty.gov/hhsa/programs/phs/office_of_vital_records_and_statistics/birth_certificates.html)
  or call (619) 692-5733. The current page lists 5530 Overland Ave, Suite 170, San Diego, CA 92123,
  weekday hours, and a fee-exempt process for people experiencing homelessness.
- ☐ Ask an advocate about the current affidavit and identity requirements.

## California ID or driver license

- ☐ Ask an advocate or [California DMV](https://www.dmv.ca.gov/) which identity/residency documents
  are accepted in your situation.
- ☐ Ask about fee waivers, reduced-fee ID, replacement, REAL ID requirements, and an appointment or
  walk-in option.
- ☐ Track application/confirmation number privately.

## Social Security card

- ☐ Use the official [Social Security replacement-card page](https://www.ssa.gov/number-card/replace-card)
  or a Social Security office. Ask what identity evidence is accepted; requirements depend on citizenship
  and status.
- ☐ Never share a Social Security number in a public form, message, or photograph.

## DD-214 / veterans records

- ☐ Ask a county veterans service officer or [VA records office](https://www.va.gov/records/get-military-service-records/)
  about a DD-214 request and safe mail destination.
- ☐ Ask about VA enrollment, benefits, transportation, and legal assistance while the record request is
  pending.
