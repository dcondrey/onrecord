# Mutual Aid / Peer-to-Peer Mediation Agreement

**A voluntary framework for a small group, pod, or shared outdoor space.** This is not a lease, security
contract, legal waiver, or substitute for emergency services. No one is required to join, disclose a
diagnosis, surrender belongings, or accept unsafe mediation.

Group/pod name: __________________________  Date adopted: ____________________

## Shared commitments

- Treat people with dignity; no threats, harassment, discrimination, retaliation, or taking belongings.
- Share supplies by an agreed method and record what is scarce without naming vulnerable people.
- Keep exits, paths, cooking areas, bathrooms, and fire access clear.
- Use agreed quiet hours and communicate changes before they become conflict.
- Use safer waste, sharps, food, pet, and hygiene practices; ask outreach for supplies and disposal.
- Protect privacy. Do not post names, health information, recovery codes, immigration information, or
  exact sleeping locations without informed consent.

## Conflict resolution

1. Anyone may call a pause and move away from a heated exchange.
2. If safe, two neutral peers listen separately, summarize the issue, and ask what outcome each person
   wants. No one is forced to meet the other person.
3. Agree on a small, specific next step and a check-in time. Write only what the parties consent to keep.
4. For threats, violence, medical crisis, fire, weapons, or exploitation, prioritize safety and contact
   emergency/outreach services. Peer mediation is not a substitute for protection.
